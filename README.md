## 🌟 What's Inside (`index.html`)

1. **Executive Diagnostic & Speaking Roadmap (`Chart.js` Radar):** Analysis of Miguel's Lesson 01 strengths (sharp ear for rhythm linking/chunking, warm small-talk curiosity) and focus areas (compressing function words in stress-timed speech, monthly Business Analyst presentations).
2. **Module 01 — The "Cats Eat Mice" Stress-Timed Rhythm Lab:** 4-level interactive rhythm trainer demonstrating how British English compresses helper words (`might've eaten` $\rightarrow$ `/ˈmaɪtəv ˈiːʔn/`) while landing on key content beats—complete with an in-browser **Microphone Voice Recorder (`MediaRecorder` API)**.
3. **Module 02 — Tadao Ando's "Church of Light", Spaces & Culture Explorer:** Interactive 3-tab explorer covering:
   - *Tadao Ando's Church of Light (Osaka, Japan):* `crucifix`, `priest / reverend`, `congregation`, `raw concrete & dark wood`, `functional` vs. `fancy / elaborate`, and `monolithic`.
   - *Favourite Spots & Nature vs. Madrid Traffic:* `tranquil` mountain streams, `spot` (place or local burger/chicken restaurant), and riding a motorbike in `hectic / stressful` city traffic (`People are driving like crazy`).
   - *Hanoi Culture & Future High-Pressure Drills:* Vietnamese family pronouns (`Anh`, `Chú`, `Cô`, `Em`, `Bác`) and our upcoming `asymmetrical` communication game (*Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes*).
4. **Module 03 — Business Analyst Monthly Presentation & "Bottleneck" Studio:** Visual breakdown of `bottleneck` (cross-department, welding vs. polishing, recruitment/onboarding) plus an interactive **4-Stage Monthly BA Update Script Builder** with one-click UK/US audio and copy-to-clipboard.
5. **Module 04 — "What You Said" $\rightarrow$ Natural Native Upgrades:** 10 side-by-side transcript cards upgrading exact moments from Lesson 01 (`fluency` vs. `frequency`, `small talk`, `into indoor climbing`, `webcam mic` vs. `micro`, `fifth` vs. `fifty`, `give a presentation in a meeting`, `these days` vs. `nowadays`, `wood` vs. `good`, `attend a meeting/church`, `stressful` vs. `distressing`).
6. **Module 05 & 06 — 24-Word Filterable Vocabulary Vault & 3D Flip Flashcards:** Searchable cards with UK/US Web Speech API audio, IPA phonetics, Spanish hints, and an interactive 3D flashcard deck.
7. **Module 07 — 8-Question Interactive Review Challenge** with `canvas-confetti` celebration.
